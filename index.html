Story Mode

Ten levels of number confidence, one step at a time.

Story Mode climbs a ten-level difficulty ladder that never jumps too far ahead. Kids keep three hearts per level, so a wrong answer is a chance to try again — not a dead end.

  • Difficulty rises gradually, so every level feels winnable
  • Three hearts per level keep mistakes low-pressure
  • Fossil Trade mini-game reinforces counting between story beats
  • Daily check-ins bring kids back without pushing screen time
For parents

Built to be safe first, fun second — never the other way around.

01

COPPA-compliant by design

No chat, no strangers, no data collection beyond what's needed to run the game. Built to comply with children's privacy law from day one.

02

Ads kids can't accidentally trigger

Rewarded ads are opt-in only, shown between levels, and never interrupt active play or link out without a clear, deliberate tap.

03

Progress that respects attention spans

Short levels, gentle daily check-ins, and no infinite-scroll mechanics — the game is designed to end a session, not extend it.
