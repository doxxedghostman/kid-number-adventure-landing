Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

01Overview

Kid Number Adventure ("the App") is developed by Wobblewing Studios and published by D&D Interiors and Construction Ltd. The App is a number-learning game designed for children ages 2–9. We built it to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy Requirements. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your rights as a parent or guardian.

02Information we collect

The App does not require account creation, sign-in, or any personal information from your child to play.

  • Game progress — stored locally on your device only (via local storage), including which worlds/levels are unlocked and hearts remaining. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.
  • Backup/restore codes — if you choose to generate a backup code to transfer progress to another device, that code is generated and used locally; we do not store it on any server.
  • No direct collection of names, email addresses, photos, location, or contacts — the App does not ask for or access any of this.

03Advertising

The App is free to play and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Ads are shown between levels and are opt-in for any rewarded content — they do not interrupt active gameplay.

Because the App is directed at children, ads are served in accordance with Google's Families Ads program, meaning:

  • No personalized/behavioral advertising based on browsing history
  • No ads for content inappropriate for children
  • AdMob may collect limited technical data (such as a device or advertising identifier) to serve and measure non-personalized ads and prevent fraud, governed by Google's Privacy Policy

04Third-party services

The only third-party service integrated into the App is Google AdMob, used solely for displaying ads as described above. We do not integrate any chat, social, analytics-for-profiling, or data-sharing SDKs beyond what's needed to serve ads.

05Children's privacy (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the locally-stored, on-device gameplay data described above. Because no personal information is transmitted to us, there is no account data for us to access, correct, or delete on our servers — everything lives on your child's device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the App's local storage or uninstalling the App.

If you believe your child has provided us with personal information through some means not described here, please contact us using the details below and we will investigate and delete it promptly.

06Data retention & deletion

Since gameplay data is stored only on-device, you control retention directly — uninstalling the App or clearing its storage permanently deletes all local progress data. We retain no copy of this data elsewhere.

07Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
